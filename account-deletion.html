PrepForge — Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 10 July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your PrepForge account and the data associated with it. PrepForge is developed by CandySoft (application ID com.candysoft.prepforge).

How to request deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, email candysofthelp@gmail.com from the email address associated with your PrepForge account, with the subject line “Delete my account”.

We will verify the request and permanently delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identity (email / Google sign-in).
  • Your profile (name, email, study goal, streak).
  • Your study data — mock-test attempts, scores, and study sessions.
  • Your saved preferences (reminders, language, timer settings).

What is retained

  • We do not retain any personal data after deletion, except where we are required to keep limited records to comply with legal obligations. Such records, if any, are kept only as long as legally required and are not used for any other purpose.
  • App content that is not personal to you (for example, the shared question bank) is not user data and is unaffected.

Guest accounts

If you used PrepForge as an anonymous guest (without signing in), your data is not tied to a personal identifier and is removed when the guest session ends or you uninstall the app.

Contact

Questions about account or data deletion? Email candysofthelp@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
